# DVR

DVR is a mixed-reality cinema for Meta Quest 3. Pick up a disc and put it in the player, or point a controller at a disc in the floating library. Films and live TV play on a screen in your room. Personal lets you browse Google Drive through a companion or open Netflix and Prime Video in their official web players.

## Open the project

Add this repository's root folder to Unity Hub. Install the editor version in `ProjectSettings/ProjectVersion.txt` with Android Build Support, the SDK, NDK and OpenJDK. Open `Assets/DVR/DVR.unity`.

## Use the cinema

- Look toward the screen and press **X** to center the station in front of you.
- Point at **Discs** to choose a source. **Eject** returns it to its cradle.
- During a film, press **B** or **Y**, or point at **Show controls**, to pause, skip ten seconds or adjust volume.
- Choose **Deck height** or **Screen position**, then move either thumbstick to adjust it.
- On **Personal**, the Google Drive badge identifies the connected account. **Search** finds a folder by name. **Folders** lets you browse My Drive, shared items and shared drives.
